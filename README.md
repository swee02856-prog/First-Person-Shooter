**Welcome to Fps Gaem!**

# What is this?

This is my 3d FPS Shooter built in Ursina Engine where you play as a bean. Yes, a bean. You have to kill the enemy beans before they kill you.

<img width="100" height="70" alt="Screenshot_2026-11-21_15-49-52" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2dc4fc3b-f7fb-4177-9463-c6dff03fa877" />

# Why did I make this?

I made this because I wanted a unique game (even though this one is generic) and I wanted to start 3d game development because 2d game development was
starting to become pretty boring for me. The games idea was found when I went outside and simply saw a pack of beans on sale at my local grocery store, I thought to myself "Why not I create a game with a bean" and so that idea was brought to this game.

The main idea is that you have to kill 10 beans to win the game, if you get hit 10 times however you die.. (dramatic). 
The beans shoot yellow bullets at you every few seconds and you have to dodge them (or just tank them). You deal 25 damage to them meanwhile they deal 10 damage to you.

# Requirements to play

**YOU CAN ONLY PLAY THIS GAME ON A LINUX OPERATING SYSTEM!**

- A GPU/Integrated Graphics that support OpenGL 3.3

# How to download

Go to the releases tab and download the latest executable release.

# Gameplay Inputs 

W - Move Forward

A - Move Left

S - Move Back

D - Move Right

M1 - Shoot

M2 (Hold) - Aim Down Sights
